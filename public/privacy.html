Contest Hunter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Contest Hunter ("the app", "we") helps you track Instagram giveaways and draft answers. This policy explains what data the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

Short version

You can use the app without an account. An account only stores your email and subscription status so Pro features can unlock across your devices. We never sell your data, and you can delete your account in the app at any time.

What we collect

  • Account information — if you create an account, we store your email address and a password hash (managed by Supabase Auth).
  • Subscription status — your plan (Free or Pro) and its status, so the app can unlock the features you paid for.
  • Contest data you track — contest links, deadlines and notes you save are stored on your own device. If you sign in, tracking may sync to your account so it survives reinstall.

We do not collect your Instagram credentials, direct messages, location, or contacts. The app only opens Instagram links with your permission.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To show the correct plan and unlock Pro features.
  • To provide AI answer drafts (only the contest text you choose is sent to the AI provider).

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share data only with the services required to run the app (Supabase for auth and storage, OpenRouter for AI answer drafts, Apple for app distribution). Each of these services is bound by its own privacy policy and only receives the data needed for its function.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from the mobile app: Profile → Account & plan → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, sign-in details and stored data. Deletion cannot be undone. If you have a Pro subscription bought on the website, cancel it there before deleting your account.

You can also request deletion by opening an issue at github.com/Ariffkmy/contest-hunter/issues.

Data retention

Account data is kept while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account or request removal. Contest tracking stored only on your device is removed when you delete the app or clear app data.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: github.com/Ariffkmy/contest-hunter/issues
